        How to play

        • 7 football prompts a day — the same for everyone.
        • You get 30 seconds to name one correct answer. Wrong guesses don't cost you, so keep trying.
        • Obvious answers score a few points. Rare answers score big: Tap-in, Routine finish, Clinical finish, Screamer, Worldie.
        • Your points climb you up the pyramid: Sunday League → Non-League → The EFL → The Premier League → the European Cup Final.
        • Spelling doesn't need to be perfect, and surnames are usually enough.
        • One daily game, once a day. Leaving mid-prompt counts that prompt as a miss. Practice rounds are unlimited and don't affect your stats.
